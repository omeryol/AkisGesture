Akış Gesture is an open-source, highly customizable navigation gesture application designed for Android and HyperOS devices.

It allows you to customize swipe gestures from the left, right, and bottom edges of your screen to perform navigation actions, switch apps, or launch custom shortcuts.

Key Features:

• Fluid Edge Gestures:
Configure swipe, swipe & hold, and double-swipe actions across multiple zones along the left, right, and bottom screen edges.

• 120 FPS Fluid Animations:
Experience 14 organic edge animations, including Aurora flow, liquid curves, and natural light effects tuned for modern high-refresh screens.

• Recent Apps Dock Menu:
Pull and hold from any edge to invoke an interactive curved dock menu for fast, zero-latency app switching.

• System Stability on HyperOS & Android 10+:
Engineered with reactive recovery mechanisms, crash protection, and frozen service monitors to keep accessibility gestures running reliably even on aggressive battery-optimizing OEM skins.

• Root & Shizuku Watchdog:
Optional, non-intrusive self-recovery via Shizuku or Root to restore accessibility services without user interruption.

• Complete Privacy & Security:
100% Free and Open Source (MIT License). Requires NO internet permission. Zero trackers, zero analytics, zero advertisements. All settings stay strictly local to your device.
